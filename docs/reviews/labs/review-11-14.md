## Summary

Labs 11 to 14 read well, the step order works and none relies on another lab's resources (all four have `needs: []`). No prices, account IDs, ARNs with digits or email addresses appear. The main gaps are cost badges and cleanup completeness. Lab 12 has no cost entries at all although it runs a Glue crawler, Athena scans and stores S3 data; lab 11 lacks CloudWatch (metric, alarm, logs) cost entries and has two cleanup steps (c3 trail, c5 buckets) whose code does not delete anything; lab 13 does not check for native DynamoDB backups or badge the tables, and its second-Region cleanup relies on a shell variable that a CloudShell restart wipes; lab 14 depends on `$BUCKET` after a day's wait and bundles three cleanup actions under one quote. Plan claims in lab 14 are properly hedged; none asserts availability without a page. Several steps go slightly beyond their quotes (Config setup cited to a start/stop page, Glue role naming, Athena result location requirement, Backup advanced-features setting).
